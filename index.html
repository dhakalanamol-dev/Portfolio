01 About

A bit of my story — and where I'm headed.

How I work

I've been curious about how things work for as long as I can remember. As a kid, I was fascinated by the AI and automated systems I saw in movies — by 5th grade, that curiosity turned into a habit of trying to build things myself. That habit carried into where I am now: a BSc CSIT student building real projects and working toward full-stack development. I learn best by building — projects and challenges, not just theory.

Outside of code

Football, gaming, and reading about business and marketing — I like understanding how things are built and how they're run. Right now I'm focused on building a strong portfolio and developing skills toward freelancing and remote opportunities. This site is part of that process.
